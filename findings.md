## Re-audit notes

- The shared report date parser treats a date-only end value as midnight at the start of that day. This can make the admin activity page and reports disagree about activity on the selected end date.
- Reports still turn response field names into table headings and CSV headers, which exposes developer-oriented wording to owners even though the underlying summaries are business-friendly.
- The terminal canvas is now readable on narrow screens and gives a clear sideways-view cue, but it remains a wide workspace rather than a truly phone-native layout. A full reflow needs a deliberate product design because existing workstations place controls on a fixed canvas.
- Terminal settings still contained owner-facing technical phrasing, displayed a terminal identifier, performed an unused connected-data lookup, and did not surface a returned public-access save error.
